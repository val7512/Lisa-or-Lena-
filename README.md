Lisa or Lena? Quiz
Is your style more Lisa or Lena? Play to find out!
This project aims to bring back the ultimate visual nostalgia from 2019. I wanted to recreate the classic "This or That" aesthetic battle games that were super popular back then, but updated with a clean, cute coquette vibe.

## How It Works
The quiz takes you through 5 rounds where you choose between Lisa and Lena aesthetics:
*  Pick your outfit
*  Pick your room
*  Pick your nails
*  Pick your fav dessert
*  Pick your Footwear
At the end, the game automatically adds up your picks and lets you know whose style match you are with a personalized message!
